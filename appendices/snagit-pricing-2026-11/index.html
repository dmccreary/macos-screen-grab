    Snagit Pricing, November 2026¶

    Snagit Individual costs $39 per user per year, billed annually, according to TechSmith's pricing page. It covers two computers, but only one can be in use at a time. These figures were checked on November 1, 2026 and will go out of date, so check TechSmith's page before relying on them.

    Annual Prices¶

    Plan Price per user per year Source
    Snagit Individual $39 TechSmith
    Business About $48 Third-party sites only
    Student About $20 Third-party sites only

    TechSmith's page has tabs for its business and education plans, but it didn't show their prices in what could be retrieved, so treat those two figures as approximate. Snagit is also included in all Camtasia plans, which start at $39 per user per year.

    Computer Limit¶

    TechSmith says each user may install and use one copy on up to two computers for their sole use, provided only one is in use at any given time. Its activation notes give the same two-machine limit for a single user.

    The two-computer wording comes from TechSmith's licensing and activation pages, some of which refer to key-based licenses. The current pricing page doesn't state a device limit, and it says subscriptions use account sign-in rather than product keys. Nothing found suggests the limit changed for subscriptions, but no current page confirms it either.

    Transfers¶

    Individual subscriptions are non-transferable. TechSmith points anyone who needs to move a license between users to a business or multi-seat plan.

    Earlier Pricing¶

    The earliest documented price found is from TechSmith's pricing and registration page, captured on October 10, 1997. A single-user copy cost $39.95, and a 10-copy multi-user pack cost $200. The price at Snagit's 1990 launch has not been found.

    The author recalls buying Snagit for about $10 many years ago. This is a personal recollection, and no receipt or published price has been found to confirm it.
