## Layout Notes

- Keep the seven icons at the positions listed. The overlay places numbered markers

  Arrows will be drawn there at runtime.

- Keep all outer lines dark enough to stay visible on a white background.
- **Reminder: no text, numbers, title, arrows, or lines between the icons.**
