# References: The Final Decision and the Capstone Project

1. [Decision matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_matrix) - Wikipedia - Describes scoring options against weighted criteria to choose among alternatives. The method behind the chapter's weighted decision matrix. Read it before building your own matrix.

2. [Cost-benefit analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost%E2%80%93benefit_analysis) - Wikipedia - Explains comparing the total expected costs and benefits of options, including uncertainty and valuation limits. Supports the chapter's cost-per-feature-used comparison.

3. [Sensitivity analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensitivity_analysis) - Wikipedia - Covers how varying inputs changes a model's output. Shows students how to test which assumption could flip their buy-or-generate decision.

4. Thinking, Fast and Slow - Daniel Kahneman - Farrar, Straus and Giroux - Kahneman documented the planning fallacy and the value of taking the outside view over a rosy inside view. Both ideas warn against trusting optimistic cost and time claims.

5. Decisions with Multiple Objectives - Ralph L. Keeney and Howard Raiffa - Cambridge University Press - Keeney and Raiffa built the formal theory of weighing several objectives with additive value functions. It is the intellectual basis for a weighted decision matrix.

6. [Performing a Project Premortem](https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem) - Gary Klein, Harvard Business Review - Klein proposes imagining the project has already failed and listing reasons why. A short technique for stress-testing a decision before committing.

7. [The Turing Way: Guide for Reproducible Research](https://book.the-turing-way.org/reproducible-research/reproducible-research) - The Turing Way - A community handbook on making results reproducible and shareable. Guides publishing the evidence behind a capstone decision. Its checklists suit a capstone repository.

8. [GitHub Pages Documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages) - GitHub Docs - Explains how to publish a static site from a repository. Supports the capstone step of publishing your evidence. It is the free hosting path used for this book.

9. [MkDocs Material: Getting Started](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/getting-started/) - Martin Donath - Shows how to install and configure the documentation framework used to build this book. Helpful for students publishing their own capstone write-up.

10. [Decision Matrix Analysis](https://www.mindtools.com/a1p0kxw/decision-matrix-analysis) - Mind Tools - A step-by-step walkthrough of building and weighing a decision matrix with a worked example. A practical template for the final decision.
