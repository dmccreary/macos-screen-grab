# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

ScreenGrab: Software Bloat and the Generated Alternative is a short course
about how a simple $9 screen grab utility bloated into a $30-per-computer
annual subscription, and how a user can now use generative AI to build a tiny
custom tool that does only the two things they need. The intended audience is
adult continuing-education learners: technically curious computer users,
students, and software professionals. The visual tone should be clean, modern,
and slightly wry: practical and frugal rather than flashy, with a hint of humor
about carrying too much baggage.

## Title

Place "ScreenGrab: Software Bloat and the Generated Alternative" in the center
of the image, in a clean, highly legible sans-serif font. Put "ScreenGrab" on
its own line in large bold type, with the subtitle "Software Bloat and the
Generated Alternative" smaller beneath it. Use a white font color with a
subtle drop shadow or a dark indigo scrim behind it so it stays readable
against the busy montage background. Do not shrink the title to fit; instead
simplify the background directly behind the text.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each rendered
in the same flat vector style (see Style below) so the composition reads as
one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles:

1. **Bloated toolbar** — a cluttered app window crammed with dozens of tiny
   unlabeled icons and menus, with a single highlighted button standing out.
2. **80/20 usage bar** — a horizontal bar chart where a 20% slice glows in
   amber and the remaining 80% is dimmed gray, labeled "80% unused" only.
3. **Price tag stack** — a "$9" tag on the left and a larger "$30 / yr" tag
   on the right, with a small arrow rising between them.
4. **Three computers** — a laptop, an iMac, and a Mac mini in a row, each with
   a small subscription renewal badge.
5. **Crosshair region selection** — a dashed rectangle with corner handles
   being dragged over a screen, with an amber crosshair cursor.
6. **Prompt to code** — a chat bubble with a short request flowing into a
   terminal window with lines of Swift code.
7. **Token meter** — a small gauge or coin stack representing tokens and a
   "$14" total cost label.
8. **Test checklist** — a clipboard with green checkmarks and one open amber
   question mark, representing what tests cannot confirm.

## Mascot

Place Pinch, the book's hermit crab mascot, in the lower-left corner, sized so
it does not overlap the title text. Pinch is a compact, chunky coral-orange
hermit crab (`#FF7043`, outline `#BF360C`) with big round eyes on two short
stalks, a friendly slightly wry smile, one large claw on the viewer's left and
one small claw on the right. Pinch carries an indigo (`#3F51B5`) spiral shell
marked with a small amber crosshair. Match the style of
`docs/img/mascot/welcome.png`.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: modern flat vector cartoon with clean, even outlines and
  no gradients. Apply it to every montage element.
- Color palette: deep indigo and navy background with coral orange (the
  mascot) and amber accents; white title text.
- Lighting/mood: bright, optimistic, a bit playful, but uncluttered at the
  center.
- Composition: title centered, montage elements arranged in a loose ring
  around it, mascot in the lower-left, generous negative space immediately
  behind the title. A subtle idea of "less is more": the montage can fade
  slightly toward the edges.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people
  pointing at whiteboards).
- Avoid photorealistic human faces.
- Do not use real product logos or brand names of existing screen grab tools.
- Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title.
