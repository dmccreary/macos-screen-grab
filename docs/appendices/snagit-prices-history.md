# Snagit Price History, 2010–2026

This table covers 17 calendar years, from the first commercial Mac release in 2010 through 2026. Prices are in **US dollars for one new individual license usable on Mac**, excluding tax, promotions, education discounts, and upgrade-only pricing. Some releases arrived in the preceding calendar year, so transition years show both prices.

Snagit for Mac entered public beta in December 2009, offered free, before its first paid release on November 9, 2010. There was no commercial Mac purchase price before that release. Earlier Windows-only prices, including the 1997 price, are excluded. See [Macworld's beta announcement](https://www.macworld.com/article/201611/snagit.html) and [commercial release announcement](https://www.macworld.com/article/208878/snagit_mac.html).

The earlier prices bought a perpetual license to that version; the recent $39 price is a recurring annual charge. Maintenance and major-version upgrades could cost extra under the older model.

This is a sourced reconstruction of advertised prices, not a complete archive of every price change. The **2024 subscription price has weaker historical evidence** than the launch prices above; its linked contemporary report is from a user discussion. Research date: November 1, 2026.

Because the table excludes promotions, it doesn't show the author's recollection of buying a one-time macOS license for around $40 on a special sale. No receipt or published sale price has been found to confirm that purchase.
