# Snagit Pricing, November 2026

Snagit Individual costs **$39 per user per year, billed annually**, according to
[TechSmith's pricing page](https://www.techsmith.com/snagit-pricing.html). It
covers **two computers**, but only one can be in use at a time. These figures were
checked on November 1, 2026 and will go out of date, so check TechSmith's page
before relying on them.

## Annual Prices

| Plan | Price per user per year | Source |
|------|-------------------------|--------|
| Snagit Individual | $39 | TechSmith |
| Business | About $48 | Third-party sites only |
| Student | About $20 | Third-party sites only |

TechSmith's page has tabs for its business and education plans, but it didn't show
their prices in what could be retrieved, so treat those two figures as approximate.
Snagit is also included in all Camtasia plans, which start at $39 per user per year.

## Computer Limit

TechSmith says each user may install and use one copy on
[up to two computers for their sole use](https://discover.techsmith.com/snagit-activation),
provided only one is in use at any given time. Its
[activation notes](https://support.techsmith.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048848831-Snagit-for-Mac-Activation-Messages)
give the same two-machine limit for a single user.

The two-computer wording comes from TechSmith's licensing and activation pages,
some of which refer to key-based licenses. The current pricing page doesn't state a
device limit, and it says subscriptions use account sign-in rather than product
keys. Nothing found suggests the limit changed for subscriptions, but no current
page confirms it either.

The author recalls using one Mac license key from around 2010 on two work computers
without problems, and doesn't recall the limit being enforced at the time. This is a
personal recollection and may not apply to current subscriptions.

## Transfers

Individual subscriptions are non-transferable. TechSmith points anyone who needs to
move a license between users to a business or multi-seat plan.

## Earlier Pricing

The earliest documented price found is from TechSmith's
[pricing and registration page](https://web.archive.org/web/19971010/http://www.techsmith.com/products/snagit/register.htm),
captured on October 10, 1997. A single-user copy for Windows cost $39.95, and a
10-copy multi-user pack cost $200. The price at Snagit's 1990 launch has not been found.

Mac versions were first sold in 2010. They were one-time purchases at $49.95 to
$62.99 until annual subscriptions replaced them in 2024, as the
[Snagit Price History](snagit-prices-history.md) shows year by year.

The author recalls buying the macOS version of Snagit as a one-time license for
around $40 on a special sale. This is a personal recollection, and no receipt or
published sale price has been found to confirm it.
